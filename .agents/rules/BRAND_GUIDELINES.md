# Foursquare Healthcare Christmas Photo Shoot — Brand & Visual Identity Guidelines

## 🎨 Official Color Palette

- **Primary Deep Evergreen Pine:** `#0B3B24` (Rich festive forest green, header/hero backdrops)
- **Holiday Cardinal Red:** `#C41E3A` (Vibrant Christmas accent, action badges, CTAs)
- **Warm Mistletoe Green:** `#1B5E20` (Secondary accents, status badges)
- **Sparkling Champagne Gold:** `#D4AF37` / `#F3C053` (Highlights, stars, countdown borders)
- **Neutral Ice White:** `#F8FAFC` / `#FFFFFF` (High-contrast reading surfaces, snowy clean cards)
- **Midnight Slate:** `#0F172A` (Charcoal dark text, footers)
- **Frosted Silver Gray:** `#E2E8F0` / `#94A3B8` (Dividers, borders, subtle winter frost cards)

---

## 🔤 Typography & Hierarchy

- **Primary Display / Headings:** `'Outfit', sans-serif` (Google Font — weights 600, 700, 800) for celebratory, modern, and warm headlines.
- **Body & UI Elements:** `'Inter', sans-serif` (Google Font — weights 400, 500, 600) for pristine clarity, clinical legibility, and high accessibility.
- **Festive Accents:** Sub-badges with subtle sparkle styling, golden borders, and winter frosted glass (`backdrop-blur-md bg-white/10`).

---

## 📸 Photography & Studio Standards

- **Lighting Setup:** Softbox diffused portrait lighting calibrated for clinical skin tones, festive red/green textiles, and reflective stethoscope metal.
- **Sanitized Festive Backdrops:**
  1. *The Evergreen Fireside Hearth:* Traditional brick fireplace, garland, warm fairy lights.
  2. *Winter Birch & Frosted Pines:* Modern silver, ice blue, and snowy white aesthetic.
  3. *North Pole Santa Workshop:* Classic toy workbench, festive tree, velvet throne for Santa.
  4. *Clean Hospital Holiday Studio:* Crisp minimalist holiday wreath with clinical badge & team focus.
- **Hygiene & Sanitation Protocol:**
  - Santa suit dry-cleaned daily and steamed between sessions.
  - Hand sanitizer stations at check-in, staging, and departure.
  - All handheld holiday props (Santa hats, wrapped gift boxes, candy cane ornaments) UV-sanitized between sessions.
- **Accessibility Guarantee:**
  - Level floor transition with 36"+ studio entrance doorways for bariatric wheelchairs and mobility scooters.
  - Dedicated attendant for gentle seated transfers if requested.
